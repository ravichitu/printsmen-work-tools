About

PrintsMen Customised Studio brings the existing editors and new product tools together. Your earlier badge projects remain compatible. The Job Assistant interprets supported commands locally; it is not generative AI. Artwork is processed in your browser. Some PrintsMen libraries and fonts require internet access.
