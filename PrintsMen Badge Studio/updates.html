Automatic installation

Checks every six hours while the studio runs. A signed release downloads automatically, then installs after all design-editor tabs are closed and five minutes without activity.

Suspended or unresponsive tabs block automatic installation. Keep the computer on during an update. Source/development folders are never automatically overwritten.

Save your work first

Export or save every open job and close your design tabs before continuing. This stops and restarts the studio. It does not uninstall or create a new installation identity.

Only releases signed by the owner's update key are accepted. Activation hosting and release hosting are separate. If no trusted feed is configured, nothing is downloaded or installed.
